3. Understanding the Port Mapping
Notice this line in your output: 80:30429/TCP. Here is what those numbers mean:

80: This is the port on the LoadBalancer. This is what you use in the browser.

30429: This is the NodePort. Kubernetes opened this high-numbered port 
on every EC2 node. The LoadBalancer sends traffic to the nodes on this port, and the nodes then route it to your pod on 8080.
